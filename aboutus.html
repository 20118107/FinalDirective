ABOUT US


We are a small independent game development team bringing Final Directive to life.

Our goal is to create an atmospheric sci-fi horror experience that combines tense survival gameplay with meaningful player decisions. We wanted to build a game where every action has consequences, where even the smallest noise can mean the difference between survival and being hunted.

Inspired by the atmosphere of classic sci-fi horror and the challenge of top-down action games, Final Directive explores the idea of turning ordinary gameplay decisions into moments of genuine tension.
