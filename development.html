GAME DEVELOPMENT


Final Directive is an evolving indie game project built around our passion for atmospheric sci-fi horror, challenging gameplay and immersive world-building.

Our development process focuses on creating a tense and rewarding experience where every mechanic has a purpose and every decision carries consequences.

Gameplay Systems

We are designing the core mechanics that define Final Directive, including the noise-based countdown, thermal detection, combat and resource management.

World & Environment

The spaceship is being developed as a hostile, abandoned environment filled with dangerous creatures, hidden resources and unexplored areas.

Characters & Enemies

From our maintenance robot protagonist to the alien creatures aboard the ship, we are developing distinctive pixel-art designs that bring the game's world to life.

Story & Atmosphere

We aim to create an unsettling sci-fi horror atmosphere through environmental storytelling, visual design and the mystery surrounding the alien infestation.

Development Progress

Our current focus is on establishing the game's foundations, refining its mechanics and developing its visual identity.

As development progresses, this site will showcase new artwork, gameplay concepts, design updates and milestones.
